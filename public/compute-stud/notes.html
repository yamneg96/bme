The course in one paragraph

Engineering problems are modelled by equations that are often impossible to solve exactly. Numerical methods replace calculus by arithmetic (e.g. new = old + slope × step) and give approximate answers. Because the answers are approximate you must always ask how wrong: measure it with true / approximate relative error, know that it comes from round-off (finite digits, IEEE 754) and truncation (cut-off series, finite step), and use the Taylor series to predict how the error shrinks (O(hⁿ)). Taylor series also give the finite-difference derivative formulas and the basis of root finding, integration and ODE solvers.
